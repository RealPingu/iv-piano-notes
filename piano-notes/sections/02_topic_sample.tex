\section{Mathematics \& Callout Boxes}

You can seamlessly integrate mathematical formulas and structured callout boxes:

\begin{equation}
    E = mc^2 \quad \text{and} \quad \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
\end{equation}

\begin{examplebox}{Example: Protocol Packet Calculation}
If the MTU is 1500 bytes and the IP header is 20 bytes with a 20-byte TCP header:
\begin{equation*}
    \text{Max Payload (MSS)} = 1500 - 20 - 20 = 1460\text{ bytes}
\end{equation*}
\end{examplebox}

\end{warningbox}
